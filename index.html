Candid/Artistic Photography

Candid photography is not just about capturing moments — at Zyago Studios, it’s about celebrating authenticity. We believe in capturing natural smiles, unposed emotions, and genuine interactions. Our team is trained to blend into your celebrations, approach each moment with creativity, and work tirelessly to help you relive those precious memories for years to come.

Traditional Photography

Traditional wedding photography is all about timeless, formal portraits. At Zyago Studios, we make sure each posed moment is captured beautifully. You’ll see classic, vibrant color photos where couples, families, and friends are looking toward the camera — all smiling and saying "Cheese!" These photos highlight rituals, group moments, and cultural significance, preserving them in an elegant, well-composed way.

Wedding Film

A wedding, we believe, is like a movie — a coming together of beautiful moments into one big happy story that becomes timeless. At Zyago Studios, we specialize in crafting cinematic wedding films that showcase emotions, traditions, and love stories with elegance. After all, isn't it important to capture this fairy-tale and preserve it forever!
